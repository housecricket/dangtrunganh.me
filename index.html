The Last 10%

Open the book →

A management novel, published one chapter a week. At a company that rents out AI agents, twelve people handle what the agents can’t, and slowly start asking what that last ten percent really is.

Each chapter puts a story next to the research behind it, from Toyota’s kaizen to psychological safety, and ends with a few questions to take back to your own work.
